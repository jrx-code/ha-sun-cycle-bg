Sun Cycle Background — day simulator

The exact palette function the card runs on your dashboard, driven here by a simulated sun elevation instead of sun.sun. Sky colors, the sun halo (color, alpha and position), ray strength, the moon and star visibility all interpolate continuously — dawn, sunrise, noon, sunset, dusk and night simply fall out of the number of degrees above the horizon.

24 h simulator

Drag the slider or press play (full day in 60 s). The sun rises from below the bottom edge and climbs; after sunset a silver moon rises on the left; stars twinkle and drift — rising at the right edge, setting at the left (sped up here ~20×; the card defaults to 30 min per screen width).

Palette anchors

Between anchors everything is linear RGB interpolation — steps are invisible, because solar elevation moves by fractions of a degree per minute.

Tuning: the palette lives in the STOPS table (same values here and in sun-cycle-bg.js): seven anchors keyed by elevation, each with sky RGB, sun halo, ray strength, star opacity and moon position.
